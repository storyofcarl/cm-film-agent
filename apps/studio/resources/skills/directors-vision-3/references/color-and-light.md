## Color psychology

**Warm (orange, gold, red, yellow)** — romance, passion, comfort, nostalgia, energy, danger, intensity; intimacy and warmth, or threatening heat. Use for love scenes, nostalgic flashbacks, domestic comfort, danger (fire/anger), energetic action.

**Cool (blue, teal, grey, silver)** — isolation, melancholy, technology, mystery, professionalism; sterility, distance, night, coldness. Use for thriller/mystery, corporate, sci-fi, isolation, night scenes, emotional distance.

**Desaturated/monochrome** — bleakness, depression, harsh reality, documentary authenticity; timelessness, severity, focus on form. Use for gritty drama, war, post-apocalyptic, historical, serious documentary.

**Highly saturated** — fantasy, heightened reality, joy, vibrancy; youthfulness, optimism, adventure. Use for animation, fantasy, musical sequences, coming-of-age, comedy, dream sequences.

**Strategic accents** — a single color pop in a muted scene creates symbolic importance and draws the eye. Red in grey = danger/blood/warning. Yellow in blue = hope/warmth in a cold world. Use sparingly for maximum impact.

## Color transition arcs

- **Warm → cool:** loss of innocence, hope fading, entering danger, emotional distancing.
- **Cool → warm:** finding connection, hope returning, resolution.
- **Desaturated → saturated:** coming alive, awakening, transformation, joy emerging.
- **Saturated → desaturated:** loss, defeat, reality setting in, disillusionment.

## Lighting as storytelling

**High key** (bright, minimal shadows) — comedy, safety, transparency, optimism. Message: nothing to hide. Comedies, idealized worlds, corporate/medical, upbeat narratives.

**Low key** (dark, deep shadows) — mystery, danger, secrets, romance (candlelight), noir. Message: secrets lurking, moral ambiguity. Thrillers, mysteries, intimate romance, hidden truths.

**Naturalistic** (motivated, realistic) — drama, realism, authenticity. Message: believable, emotionally honest. Dramas, character studies, documentary-style.

**Stylized** (unmotivated, artistic) — genre cinema, heightened reality, emotion over realism. Music videos, fantasy, expressionism, genre films.

## Lighting patterns

- **Three-point** (key + fill + rim) — dimensional, polished, traditional.
- **Single source** (key only, deep shadows) — mystery, danger, noir.
- **Backlight dominant** (silhouette) — mystery, anonymity.
- **Practical dominant** (lamps, windows, screens) — grounded, authentic, modern.
- **Colored** (neon, gels, motivated color) — genre, mood, heightened emotion.
