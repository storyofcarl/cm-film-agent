## THRILLER / SUSPENSE

**Color:**
- Desaturated, cool blues and greys dominate
- Occasional warm accents signal threat or clues
- Muted palette emphasizes realism and tension

**Lighting:**
- High contrast with deep shadows
- Motivated darkness (practical sources)
- Shadows conceal and reveal strategically

**Camera:**
- Dutch angles create unease
- Slow push-ins build tension
- Tight framing creates claustrophobia
- Low angles emphasize threat

**Movement:**
- Deliberate, creeping movements
- Sudden reveals for shock
- Restrained until payoff moments

**Pacing:**
- Held tension shots (3-5s)
- Quick cuts on action beats
- Rhythm: slow build, sudden release

**Grade:**
- Crushed blacks for mystery
- Teal-orange separation (thriller standard)
- Clinical, controlled look

**Audio:**
- Sparse, minimal soundscape
- Silence used as weapon
- Sudden impacts for scares

## HORROR

**Color:**
- Desaturated or monochrome base
- Blood red accents (only color in scene)
- Darkness emphasized

**Lighting:**
- Extreme low key (very dark)
- Practical sources only (realistic motivation)
- Darkness itself is threat

**Camera:**
- Low angles make threats loom
- Static shots create dread (anticipation)
- Sudden movements shock audience
- Wide lenses distort, create unease

**Movement:**
- Slow reveals (building dread)
- Darkness-to-light reveals terror
- Stillness more frightening than movement

**Pacing:**
- Very slow builds (5-8s holds)
- Sudden shock cuts
- Rhythm: anticipation, then terror

**Grade:**
- Heavy vignette (darkness closing in)
- Film grain for texture and unease
- Crushed blacks (hide the monster)

**Audio:**
- Ambient dread (low rumbles, distant sounds)
- Silence before scares
- Visceral impacts for jump scares

---

## ROMANCE / DRAMA

**Color:**
- Warm golden tones, soft pastels
- Amber and honey tones
- Inviting, emotional warmth

**Lighting:**
- Soft, diffused quality
- Golden hour aesthetic
- Candlelight intimacy
- Gentle, flattering light

**Camera:**
- Close-ups for emotional intimacy
- Slow, graceful movements
- Eye-level connection between characters
- Frames emphasize togetherness

**Movement:**
- Slow push-ins (drawing into emotion)
- Drifting, floating quality
- Graceful, deliberate pacing

**Pacing:**
- Longer takes (3-6s)
- Let emotional moments breathe
- Hold on reactions and realizations

**Grade:**
- Slight glow/diffusion
- Soft contrast (not harsh)
- Warm highlights, gentle shadows

**Audio:**
- Intimate sounds (breathing, fabric, touch)
- Emotional musical score
- Environmental romance (rain, fire, wind)

---

## COMEDY

**Color:**
- Saturated, bright, cheerful
- Warm and inviting palette
- Clear, readable colors

**Lighting:**
- High key (bright, even)
- No mystery or shadows
- Everything visible and clear

**Camera:**
- Medium shots for physical comedy
- Eye-level for relatability
- Clear framing, nothing obscured

**Movement:**
- Snappy, energetic
- Quick reframes on gags
- Follows action clearly

**Pacing:**
- Quick cuts (1-2s)
- Rapid-fire timing
- Rhythmic editing for jokes

**Grade:**
- Bright, punchy colors
- Clear visibility always
- No mood obscuring comedy

**Audio:**
- Clear dialogue priority
- Comedic sound effects
- Upbeat, light music

---

## ACTION

**Color:**
- High saturation during action
- May desaturate in aftermath (contrast)
- Bold, clear colors

**Lighting:**
- High contrast for drama
- Dramatic but visible action
- Practical motivated sources

**Camera:**
- Dynamic, changing angles
- Low angles for power and impact
- Dutch angles for chaos
- Wide shots establish geography
- Close shots show impact

**Movement:**
- Handheld energy and immediacy
- Whip pans between action
- Quick reframes
- Camera shake on impacts

**Pacing:**
- Very quick cuts (0.5-1.5s)
- Longer on establishing shots
- Rhythm: staccato, energetic

**Grade:**
- Punchy contrast
- Saturated colors
- Impact frames emphasized

**Audio:**
- Layered impact sounds
- Motion whooshes
- Music-driven energy
- Powerful bass on hits

## FILM NOIR

**Color:**
- Monochrome or heavily desaturated
- Single color accent (often red)
- Black and white mentality

**Lighting:**
- Venetian blind patterns (classic)
- Harsh single source lighting
- Silhouettes and deep shadows
- Smoke and atmosphere

**Camera:**
- Dutch angles (moral ambiguity)
- Low angles (threatening)
- Extreme high angles (surveillance)
- Dramatic compositions

**Movement:**
- Deliberate, prowling
- Reveals through shadow play
- Stalking quality

**Pacing:**
- Medium holds (2-4s)
- Punctuated by quick cuts on violence
- Methodical rhythm

**Grade:**
- Deep blacks, bright highlights
- No mid-tones (high contrast)
- Heavy contrast, graphic look

**Audio:**
- Jazz score
- Rain, urban night sounds
- Footsteps echoing
- Femme fatale voice-over

---

## SCI-FI / CYBERPUNK

**Color:**
- Cool blues as base
- Neon accents (pink, cyan, purple)
- Teal-orange color grading

**Lighting:**
- Practical screens and monitors
- Neon lighting (motivated by world)
- Harsh fluorescent
- Colored gels for atmosphere

**Camera:**
- Wide lenses show environment scale
- Slow reveals of technology
- Establishing shots show world

**Movement:**
- Smooth, gliding (futuristic)
- Deliberate, controlled
- Technology-enabled moves

**Pacing:**
- Varies: slow world-building, quick action
- Holds on technology reveals

**Grade:**
- Cool, technological palette
- Neon saturation
- Contrasty, stylized

**Audio:**
- Electronic soundscapes
- Technology hums and beeps
- Synthesized music
- Urban future ambience

**Movement:**
- Graceful, floating quality
- Otherworldly smoothness
- Dream-like pacing

**Grade:**
- Saturated, rich colors
- Magical color enhancements
- Dreamy, enhanced look

**Audio:**

- Magical sound effects
- Environmental wonder sounds

## WESTERN

**Color:**
- Warm, dusty earth tones
- Golden hour dominance
- Desaturated dusty palette

**Lighting:**
- Harsh, natural sunlight
- Deep shadows in midday
- Golden hour magic

**Camera:**
- Wide shots (landscape important)
- Eye-level confrontations
- Leone-style extreme close-ups

**Movement:**
- Deliberate, purposeful
- Slow approach to confrontation
- Static during showdowns

**Pacing:**
- Slow build to action
- Quick on gunfire
- Holds on landscape (4-6s)

**Grade:**
- Warm, dusty
- Slightly desaturated
- Film grain texture

**Audio:**
- Wind, natural sounds
- Sparse dialogue
- Iconic western score
- Silence in showdowns

## DOCUMENTARY / REALISM

**Color:**
- Natural, as-found colors
- No stylization
- True-to-life palette

**Lighting:**
- Available light only
- Natural, motivated sources
- Imperfect, realistic

**Camera:**
- Handheld, observational
- Eye-level, neutral
- No dramatic angles

**Movement:**
- Following action naturally
- Reactive, not choreographed
- Imperfect, human operator feel

**Pacing:**
- Longer takes (4-8s)
- Real-time or near real-time
- Observational rhythm

**Grade:**
- Minimal grading
- Natural look maintained
- No stylization

**Audio:**
- Natural, ambient sound
- Direct dialogue recording
- Environmental authenticity
- Minimal music (or none)

## Mixing Genres

Many projects blend genres. When mixing:

1. **Identify dominant genre** for baseline visual language
2. **Layer secondary genre elements** for complexity
3. **Use contrast strategically** - switching between genre looks for narrative effect
4. **Maintain coherence** - don't randomly switch, make changes meaningful

**Example: Sci-Fi Horror**
- Base: Sci-Fi cool palette and tech lighting
- Add: Horror darkness, extreme shadows, dread pacing
- Result: Dark, technological dread (Alien-style)

**Example: Romantic Comedy**
- Base: Comedy bright, high-key lighting
- Add: Romance warm tones, intimate close-ups
- Result: Warm, bright, emotionally intimate comedy

**Example: Action Thriller**
- Base: Thriller cool tones, tension building
- Add: Action quick cuts, dynamic camera
- Result: Fast-paced tension with explosive releases
