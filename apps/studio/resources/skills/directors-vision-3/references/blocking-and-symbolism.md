# Blocking & Symbolism

## Contents
- Position = Power
- Spacing = Relationship
- Movement = Intention
- Visual metaphors & symbolism

## Position = Power

- **Dominant:** higher in frame, center frame, backlit silhouette, facing camera, closer to camera.
- **Submissive:** lower in frame, edge of frame, fully lit (exposed), back to camera, further from camera.

## Spacing = Relationship

- **Intimacy:** close proximity, shared frame, touching or near-touching.
- **Tension:** close but not touching, diagonal opposition.
- **Distance:** separated by space, objects between, or in different shots entirely.
- **Power struggle:** meeting center frame, equal framing, both strong positions.
- **Isolation:** alone in frame, others out of frame or blurred.

## Movement = Intention

- **Toward camera:** approaching, threatening, engaging, advancing.
- **Away from camera:** retreating, leaving, rejection, withdrawal.
- **Across frame:** journey, transition, searching, lateral movement.
- **Static:** stability, contemplation, stubbornness, paralysis.
- **Circling:** stalking, dancing, negotiation, confrontation, predatory.

## Visual metaphors & symbolism

- **Windows/frames within frame:** trapped, observed, separated, barriers.
- **Mirrors/reflections:** duality, self-perception, truth vs. appearance, identity.
- **Shadows:** hidden self, danger lurking, the past haunting, dual nature.
- **Light shafts:** hope, revelation, spiritual moment, breakthrough.
- **Rain/water:** cleansing, sadness, renewal, rebirth, washing away.
- **Fire:** passion, destruction, warmth, danger, transformation, anger.
- **Empty spaces:** loneliness, loss, unfulfilled potential, absence, void.
