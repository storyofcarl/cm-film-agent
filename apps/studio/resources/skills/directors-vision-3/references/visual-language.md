## Medium: animation, live action, hybrid

State the medium early; it shapes every downstream choice. Default to an animation register unless the project is live-action or hybrid.

- **Animation** — specify render/look target (cel-shaded, painterly, 3D-rendered, 2D-traditional, anime-influenced, stop-motion feel), line and shading language, frame cadence (on 1s/2s/3s), and how stylization serves tone. Color and light can be fully authored.
- **Live action** — naturalistic motivation matters more; grades and stylization layer onto captured reality. The expressive techniques here (color arcs, lighting moods, symbolic framing) apply fully to live action.
- **Hybrid** — define where the seam sits (live plates with animated elements, rotoscope, animated sequences inside a live film) and how the registers reconcile.

Record the medium decision and its visual consequences in the Visual Thesis.

## Shot Size = Intimacy

- **ECU/CU** — emotional intensity, vulnerability, secrets, important details. Use for intimate moments and emotional peaks.
- **MCU/MS** — conversation, human connection, comfortable distance. Use for dialogue and character introductions.
- **MWS/WS** — context, relationships, power dynamics, spatial awareness. Use for establishing relationships within an environment.
- **EWS** — isolation, scale, insignificance, grandeur. Use for establishing locations and showing isolation or scale.

## Camera Angle = Power

- **Low angle** (up at subject) — power, dominance, threat, awe, heroic. Authority, intimidation.
- **Eye level** — equality, neutrality, realism, relatability. Normal conversation, documentary feel.
- **High angle** (down at subject) — vulnerability, weakness, submission, surveillance. Powerlessness, sympathy.
- **Dutch/canted** — disorientation, unease, instability, wrongness. Psychological disturbance, horror, tension.

## Camera Movement = Emotion

- **Static/locked** — observation, tension building, stability, control. Dramatic moments, suspense.
- **Slow push in** — discovery, increasing focus, intensification, drawing in. Revelations, intimacy.
- **Pull back/reveal** — revelation, context, distancing, loss. Surprising reveals, consequences.
- **Pan/tilt** — following action, searching, revealing space. Tracking movement, connecting elements.
- **Handheld** — urgency, realism, chaos, intimacy, immediacy. Action, documentary realism, subjective POV.
- **Smooth glide** (dolly, steadicam) — dream-like, elegant, cinematic grace. Fantasy, elegant moments, establishing.
- **Whip pan** — energy, surprise, disorientation, comedic timing. Transitions, reactions, fast-paced beats.

## Focus = Attention Direction

- **Shallow DoF** — isolates subject, intimacy, directs attention. Portraits, intimate moments, beauty shots.
- **Deep focus** — everything sharp, environmental storytelling, spatial relationships. Complex scenes where all elements matter.
- **Rack focus** — shifts attention between elements, reveals connections. Shifting perspective, revelatory moments.

## Function = What the shot does to the audience

Independent of size, angle, and movement, every beat performs one primary **function** — what it does to the viewer's attention and tension. The downstream battery checks that every beat declares a function and that it matches the beat's narrative role. Pick one primary per beat; a beat may note a secondary.

- **intensify** — raise arousal or tension. Means: push-in, accelerating cuts, tightening size, rising contrast.
- **isolate** — direct all attention to one element, strip context. Means: CU/ECU, shallow focus, negative space, silence.
- **reveal** — deliver new information. Means: pull-back, rack focus, the cut to the withheld thing, a reorienting wide.
- **reorient** — re-establish space, time, or relationship after a disturbance. Means: re-establishing wide, match cut, clarifying geography.
- **release** — discharge accumulated tension. Means: cut to wide/air, the held resolving frame, the exhale after a peak.

These name the *effect*, leaving execution open — the separation that lets the framework carry any director's taste. They align with the experiential levers (attention, tension, comprehension) the pipeline steers.
