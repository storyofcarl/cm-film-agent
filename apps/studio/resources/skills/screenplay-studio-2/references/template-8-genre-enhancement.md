## GENRE-SPECIFIC ENHANCEMENT GUIDELINES

### COMEDY ENHANCEMENT

#### Character Enhancement Strategies
- **Intensify Character Flaws**: Make comedic character traits more pronounced
- **Develop Distinctive Voices**: Give characters unique speech patterns/perspectives
- **Create Comic Foils**: Ensure character combinations create friction and contrast
- **Establish Running Behaviors**: Develop signature actions or responses
- **Layer Comedic Motivation**: Add secondary objectives that create complications

#### Scene Enhancement Techniques
- **Plant Visual Gags**: Add physical comedy elements to scenes
- **Escalate Situations**: Build complications in logical but surprising ways
- **Heighten Contrast**: Increase difference between expectation and reality
- **Add Callbacks**: Reference earlier moments for comedic payoff
- **Create Misunderstandings**: Develop moments where characters misinterpret each other

#### Comedy-Specific Metrics
- **Laugh Density**: Major comedic moments per page (target varies by comedy subgenre)
- **Comedy Variety**: Balance of physical, verbal, character, and situational humor
- **Running Gag Development**: Number of setups and callbacks for recurring jokes
- **Comic Timing**: Strategic placement of reveals and punchlines
- **Relief Moments**: Humor placement after tension for maximum impact

### THRILLER ENHANCEMENT

#### Character Enhancement Strategies
- **Deepen Stakes**: Strengthen character's personal connection to outcome
- **Create Vulnerability**: Add specific weaknesses antagonists can exploit
- **Develop Determination**: Show increasing resolve despite mounting obstacles
- **Add Specialized Knowledge**: Give characters unique skills that create tension
- **Increase Unpredictability**: Make certain characters less predictable

#### Scene Enhancement Techniques
- **Plant Red Herrings**: Add misleading clues or suspicious elements
- **Create Time Pressure**: Introduce or tighten deadlines
- **Increase Environmental Threats**: Make settings more dangerous or unstable
- **Add Near Misses**: Create moments where characters nearly discover something
- **Develop Dramatic Irony**: Let audience know dangers characters don't see

#### Thriller-Specific Metrics
- **Suspense Duration**: Length of tension-building sequences
- **Threat Escalation**: Progressive increase in danger throughout script
- **Misdirection Effectiveness**: Quality of red herrings and false leads
- **Question/Answer Ratio**: Balance of questions raised vs. answered per sequence
- **Relief Timing**: Strategic placement of tension breaks

### DRAMA ENHANCEMENT

#### Character Enhancement Strategies
- **Deepen Internal Conflict**: Strengthen character's inner struggles
- **Complicate Relationships**: Add nuance to interpersonal dynamics
- **Develop Subtext**: Create layers of unspoken meaning in dialogue
- **Add Moral Complexity**: Make character choices more ethically ambiguous
- **Strengthen Transformational Moments**: Emphasize scenes of character change

#### Scene Enhancement Techniques
- **Heighten Emotional Stakes**: Increase what characters stand to lose
- **Add Thematic Resonance**: Connect scene conflicts to central themes
- **Create Meaningful Symbolism**: Use objects or settings with symbolic significance
- **Develop Silent Moments**: Add beats where emotion is conveyed without dialogue
- **Strengthen Turning Points**: Make character decisions more impactful

#### Drama-Specific Metrics
- **Emotional Range**: Variety of emotional states experienced by characters
- **Relationship Complexity**: Depth and evolution of character connections
- **Thematic Integration**: How effectively scenes embody central themes
- **Catharsis Moments**: Number and impact of emotional release points
- **Subtextual Density**: Amount of meaning conveyed beyond literal dialogue

### ACTION ENHANCEMENT

#### Character Enhancement Strategies
- **Clarify Physical Capabilities**: Establish what characters can and cannot do
- **Add Signature Moves**: Give characters distinctive fighting styles or techniques
- **Develop Resourcefulness**: Show creative problem-solving under pressure
- **Create Physical Vulnerabilities**: Give characters specific weaknesses
- **Strengthen Determination**: Show willingness to endure increasing punishment

#### Scene Enhancement Techniques
- **Vary Action Types**: Mix different styles of action sequences
- **Create Environmental Interaction**: Use settings as active elements in action
- **Add Progressive Complications**: Make sequences increasingly difficult
- **Develop Clarity of Stakes**: Make immediate danger and objectives clear
- **Enhance Visualization**: Add specific details that make action easy to picture

#### Action-Specific Metrics
- **Action Density**: Distribution of action sequences throughout script
- **Escalation Pattern**: How action intensity increases throughout story
- **Recovery Realism**: Plausibility of character recovery between sequences
- **Stakes Progression**: How consequences of failure increase with each sequence
- **Spectacle Balance**: Mix of practical action and larger set pieces

## IMPLEMENTATION STRATEGIES

### Enhancement Process
1. **Analyze**: Identify genre-specific strengths and weaknesses in current script
2. **Prioritize**: Determine highest-impact enhancements to implement
3. **Plan**: Create specific implementation strategy for each enhancement
4. **Execute**: Make targeted revisions by chunk
5. **Review**: Assess effectiveness of enhancements and adjust as needed

### Common Enhancement Pitfalls
1. **Genre Overload**: Adding too many genre elements at once
2. **Tonal Inconsistency**: Implementing enhancements that clash with established tone
3. **Character Distortion**: Changing character behavior solely for genre requirements
4. **Forced Conventions**: Including genre elements that don't serve the specific story
5. **Pace Disruption**: Adding elements that interrupt narrative flow

### Best Practices
1. **Maintain Character Truth**: Genre enhancements should extend, not contradict, character
2. **Serve the Story**: Every enhancement should strengthen the core narrative
3. **Organic Integration**: Add genre elements that feel natural within the world
4. **Balanced Application**: Distribute enhancements throughout screenplay
5. **Consistent Tone**: Ensure all enhancements align with established tone
