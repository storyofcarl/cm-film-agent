## NARRATIVE RICHNESS ASSESSMENT GUIDELINES

### Character Arc Evaluation Criteria
1. **Completeness**: Does each character complete their intended arc?
2. **Believability**: Are state changes motivated and earned?
3. **Visibility**: Are changes clearly demonstrated through action/dialogue?
4. **Impact**: Do changes meaningfully affect the story?
5. **Resonance**: Do arcs connect to theme and central conflict?

### Setup/Payoff Quality Metrics
1. **Recognition**: Will the audience recognize payoffs when they occur?
2. **Satisfaction**: Do payoffs deliver emotional impact?
3. **Timing**: Are setups and payoffs properly spaced?
4. **Integration**: Do setups feel natural within the narrative?
5. **Balance**: Is there an appropriate mix of setup types?

### Tension Management Evaluation
1. **Variation**: Does tension rise and fall appropriately?
2. **Progression**: Does overall tension escalate toward climax?
3. **Peaks and Valleys**: Are high-tension moments balanced with breathers?
4. **Diversity**: Are different types of tension utilized?
5. **Pacing**: Is tension managed to maintain engagement?

### Scene Function Balance Assessment
1. **Appropriate Distribution**: Are functions balanced for story type?
2. **Efficiency**: Does each scene serve multiple functions?
3. **Variety**: Is there sufficient variation in consecutive scenes?
4. **Purpose**: Does each scene have clear purpose?
5. **Position**: Are functions appropriate to story position?

### Plot Dynamics Effectiveness Criteria
1. **Surprise**: Do twists genuinely surprise while remaining credible?
2. **Impact**: Do reversals significantly affect character journeys?
3. **Structure**: Are major turning points properly positioned?
4. **Motivation**: Do plot developments arise from character choices?
5. **Coherence**: Do all dynamics serve the central narrative?

## CONDUCTING AN EFFECTIVE AUDIT

### When to Perform Full Audits
1. After completing first draft of full screenplay
2. After implementing major revisions
3. Before final polish
4. When screenplay feels "flat" or "missing something"

### Process Recommendations
1. **Be Quantitative**: Use numbers and percentages where possible
2. **Be Specific**: Reference exact scenes and page numbers
3. **Be Constructive**: Phrase issues as opportunities for improvement
4. **Be Comprehensive**: Examine all narrative elements systematically
5. **Be Solutions-Oriented**: Offer specific fixes for identified issues

### Prioritization Framework
- **Highest Priority**: Issues that break story logic or character consistency
- **Medium Priority**: Elements that weaken emotional impact or clarity
- **Enhancement Opportunities**: Elements that could elevate good to great

### Implementation Approach
1. Address highest priority fixes first
2. Implement changes by chunk to maintain consistency
3. Re-audit after significant revisions
4. Track improvements against initial audit metrics
