## SCENE FUNCTION CODES

- **EXP**: Exposition - Provides background information or context
- **DEV**: Character Development - Reveals or deepens character traits/relationships
- **CON**: Conflict - Features significant conflict between characters or forces
- **REV**: Revelation - Reveals important information to characters or audience
- **PLA**: Plot Advancement - Moves the main storyline forward
- **SET**: Setup - Establishes elements that will pay off later
- **PAY**: Payoff - Delivers on previously established setups
- **TUR**: Turn/Twist - Changes story direction or expectations
- **CLI**: Climax - Represents peak moment of sequence or act
- **RES**: Resolution - Resolves a storyline element or conflict
- **REL**: Relationship Development - Focuses on evolving character dynamics

## TENSION LEVEL GUIDELINES

- **1-2**: Minimal tension - Calm, reflective, or mundane moments
- **3-4**: Low tension - Character building or setup moments with mild conflict
- **5-6**: Moderate tension - Clear conflict or obstacle present
- **7-8**: High tension - Significant conflict with important stakes
- **9-10**: Extreme tension - Critical moments, climactic confrontations
