## Creative take

A keeper rescues the final light of a closing observatory. Sixty-eight seconds,
two scenes in one room, five shots, one recurring character and one story prop.
Restrained tactile science fiction: amber practicals, cold window light, patient
camera movement. No spoken dialogue. Room tone and subtle mechanical sound support
the picture. Dialogue, multilingual speech and lip synchronization need a later
dedicated test; this pilot does not prove those capabilities.

The first scene is night. The second is later, at dawn; the time change creates a
new scene without requiring a different location asset. The source package includes
all three reusable assets: keeper, room geography and signal recorder.

## Review and execution sequence

1. **Preparation review:** accept or revise the source, five-shot coverage, reusable
   assets, global style and model settings as one deliverable. If actual completed
   work is supplied, run its completeness validation and preserve accepted elements.
2. **Representative asset lookdev:** prepare one character and one location together.
   Review the compiled prompts, references and current cost estimate before approving
   that batch. The director reviews the resulting looks. Technical success alone
   does not clear this gate.
3. **Full asset batch:** reuse accepted lookdev versions; generate only the remaining
   needed asset work. Review all three assets together and explicitly approve the
   exact versions that may become production references.
4. **Technical video lookdev:** SC-001 has four segments, so one segment is required
   by default. SC-002 has one segment and does not need a separate lookdev. Review
   the global style, compiled prompt, reference use, camera behavior and output
   technically and visually. The director may choose different coverage. Retain a
   usable approved first segment toward the finished shot.
5. **Production batch:** prepare the remaining complete picture. With successful
   lookdev reuse this is four remaining source segments. Independent work can run
   concurrently; SH-001's continuation must use its accepted predecessor. The
   director reviews all five shot versions and both assembled scenes.
6. **One-shot repair:** use a genuine defect or a clearly requested small change in
   SH-004. The crew proposes frame repair, a video edit or a full rerun with its
   estimate; the director approves the proposed revision batch once. Replace only
   SH-004 and preserve SH-005's existing source range from their shared segment.
   A 3-second standalone rerun under this profile needs 2 seconds of removable tail,
   then a 3-second trim. Keep the prior version and exact recipe accessible.
7. **Finish and deliver:** after scene approval, prepare finishing at 1080p, using
   upscale or a supported same-recipe V2V operation. Review the finished versions,
   approve the relevant scenes again when selections change, render the picture,
   and inspect the media plus JSON/OTIO export in the director's editorial workflow.
